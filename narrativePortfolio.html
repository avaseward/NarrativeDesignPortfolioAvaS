The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Beat Sheet

This project is a document consisting of the beats throughout the first dungeon in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. This demonstrates an understanding of what a beat is, how to develop a beat sheet, and how to identify a beat in a story. This project has helped my skills in developing my own beat sheets for my own narrative projects.

OMORI Horror Audio Logs

This document contains my own writing of different audio logs as a fan project for the game OMORI. These logs take information from the story of OMORI and puts this information in different areas of the world, which affects the story of the game and how Sunny learns the truth. This project demonstrates writing skills with information already given.

Steampunk Zelda Story Bible

In this document, I have written a short story bible of a steampunk world for The Legend of Zelda. This story takes inspiration from the fundamentals of the Zelda series, but the rest of the story is completely original. This demonstrates story building skills and writing skills.
